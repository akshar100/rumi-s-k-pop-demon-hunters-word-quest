Rumi's K-Pop Demon Hunters Word Quest (PWA v2.0.0)
===================================================
Included Files:
- index.html (Complete mobile-first game with Rumi, Mira, Zoey & 200 emoji sentences)
- manifest.json (Installable PWA Web App Manifest)
- sw.js (Offline-first Service Worker with automatic cache updates)
- icons/icon-192.png & icons/icon-512.png (Custom K-Pop Demon Hunters app icons)
- _headers & vercel.json (Zero-config hosting headers for Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or Vercel)

How to Host Online in 30 Seconds:
1. Drag and drop this unzipped folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop, Vercel, or GitHub Pages.
2. Open the live HTTPS link on any phone or tablet to play online, share the link, or click "Add to Home Screen" for full offline play!
